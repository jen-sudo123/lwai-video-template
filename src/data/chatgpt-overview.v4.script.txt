ChatGPT is no longer just the AI chatbot everyone has heard of.

It's becoming the operating system for how leaders write, research, analyze, automate, and get work done.

[pause] Let's start with the key features.

Search retrieves live information from the web. Deep Research goes further, synthesizing many sources into a structured, cited report.

Voice lets you speak naturally with ChatGPT. And with Advanced Voice in the mobile apps, eligible subscribers can also share live video and their screen.

Image generation creates mock-ups, diagrams, posters, and other visuals, with strong text rendering and instruction following.

Projects organize your chats, files, and instructions around ongoing work.

Skills define how to carry out a recurring task.

Apps connect services like Google Drive, Microsoft 365, Slack, and GitHub, within the permissions you set.

And Plugins bring it together. A plugin can contain skills, apps, or both. What's available depends on your plan and workspace settings.

For larger tasks, there's ChatGPT Work.

It can research, analyze information, work across your connected apps and files, and produce finished documents, spreadsheets, presentations, reports, and Sites.

Along the way, you can monitor its progress, answer its questions, redirect it, and approve important actions.

For software development, Codex provides a dedicated workspace for writing, reviewing, debugging, and shipping code.

On desktop, ChatGPT brings Chat, Work, and Codex into a more unified experience, while keeping Codex as its own workspace.

[pause] Next, the models.

ChatGPT offers different models and thinking settings. What you see depends on whether you're in Chat, Work, or Codex, and on your plan and workspace settings.

In Chat, the thinking settings are Instant, Medium, High, Extra High, and Pro.

Instant gives fast responses for straightforward tasks.

Medium, High, and Extra High allow more thinking, for tasks with several connected steps or competing constraints.

And Pro is designed for particularly demanding work.

When you select GPT-6, GPT-6 Sol powers Instant through Extra High.

GPT-6 Pro is powered by GPT-6 Astra, which also supports demanding work in ChatGPT Work and Codex. Access and usage limits vary by plan.

[pause] Now, a word on privacy.

On personal plans, you can manage training and data-sharing preferences in Settings.

For Business, Enterprise, and Edu, OpenAI says it doesn't use organizational data to train its models by default.

Whatever your plan, avoid sharing sensitive information, connect only the apps you trust, and always verify important outputs.

[pause] Finally, plans and pricing.

Free covers everyday chat and Search, with limited access to tools like file uploads, data analysis, image generation, Voice, and Deep Research.

Go raises the limits for everyday use, including uploads, image generation, and Voice.

Plus adds advanced reasoning, and expands access to tools like Deep Research, Codex, and ChatGPT Work.

Pro is built for heavier use, with higher usage allowances and access to more demanding reasoning options.

Business and Enterprise support organizational use, with shared workspaces, administration, security, and governance controls.

Plans, pricing, and model access change often, so check the official ChatGPT pricing page for the latest details.
