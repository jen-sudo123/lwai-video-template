Gemini is Google's AI Assistant.
You can chat with it, upload files,
and use voice and camera to get help
with writing, analyzing, researching, and creating.
And the big advantage is that if your work
already lives in Google,
Gemini is designed to meet you there.
It operates in two main ways.
First, there's Gemini as a chat assistant.
You can ask questions, brainstorm ideas,
summarize information, generate images,
analyze files, and do research
similar to tools like ChatGPT.
And second, there's Gemini Inside Google Workspace.
If your organization runs on Google Workspace,
Gemini appears where work already happens
across apps like Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive, and Meet.
In terms of key features,
Gemini often matches ChatGPT in range,
but with native integration across Google's ecosystem.
Depending on your plan, you may have access to search,
which includes web search with source-specific filters,
plus Q&A grounded in your workspace data.
There's also deep research,
which creates comprehensive, cited reports
using web sources, workspace context, or both.
Gemini Live supports real-time voice conversations
with optional camera or screen sharing,
so Gemini can respond to what you're showing or saying.
For image creation,
Nano Banana 2 is a high-quality image generator
and photo editor with strong text rendering
and up to 4K output.
Gemini Omni is a new model for generating and editing video
from blended text, audio, image, and video inputs.
Notebook LM turns internal sources
into a structured knowledge base
and can generate outputs like presentation feedback,
slide decks, and mind maps.
And now you can access Notebook LM directly in Gemini
with the Notebook feature.
We also have Gems, which are specialized assistants
you can build to standardize repeat workflows
and reduce repetitive prompting.
Workspace Studio supports Agentec Automation,
helping orchestrate multi-step processes
across workspace apps.
Canvas gives you a dedicated space
to create and iterate on documents and code
with export paths to docs or even live web apps.
Scheduled actions can automate recurring or future AI prompts,
such as daily reports, research tasks, or email summaries.
Coming soon is Gemini Spark,
a 24-7 personal AI agent designed to work in the background
across workspace, connectors, and the open web.
You'll be able to delegate multi-step tasks,
set recurring work, and teach it new skills.
And for developers, code assist anti-gravity
and Firebase support workflows from vibe coding
through deploying full-stack apps.
A practical advantage, especially on higher tiers,
is that Gemini supports a context window
of up to 1 million tokens.
That's useful for reasoning over
or creating long documents.
Now, in terms of models,
the Gemini 3 family includes Gemini 3.5 Flash,
Google's strongest Agentec model to date.
Gemini 3.5 Pro is currently in testing
and expected in June, 2026.
In the Gemini apps, these typically appear
as fast, thinking, and pro.
On privacy and data use,
consumer Gemini users can manage Gemini apps activity
and choose whether interactions help improve Google AI,
although Google notes some processing
may still occur for safety.
For Google workspace customers with Gemini protections,
submissions aren't used to train models,
aren't reviewed by humans,
and remain within workspace enterprise security
and admin controls.
Finally, pricing varies by plan.
Free Gemini offers general AI and web help,
including access to Gemini 3.5 Flash
with limits on advanced models and features.
For individuals, Google AI Plus is $7.99 per month.
Pro is $19.99 per month,
and Ultra starts from $99.99 per month.
For organizations, workspace plans include Gemini capabilities,
starting with business starter at $7 per user per month,
business standard at $14,
and business plus at $22 build annually.
One important detail, workspace Gemini access
depends on admin settings and licensing,
so the full in-app experience
depends on what your admin has enabled.
