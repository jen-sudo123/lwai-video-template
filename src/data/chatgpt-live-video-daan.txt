Sometimes the fastest way to use AI
is not to explain the situation, but to show it.
Video and screen sharing are useful
when the work is easier to demonstrate than describe.
Instead of writing a long prompt,
you can point ChatGPT at a slide, dashboard, landing page,
or workflow and ask it to help you observe, diagnose,
and decide what to do next.
Here's how it works.
To use live video or screen sharing,
open the ChatGPT mobile app.
You need to be signed in and on a paid plan.
Then tap the voice icon in the bottom right corner
of the screen.
Once the voice conversation starts, tap the plus icon.
From there, you can choose live video,
which turns on your camera,
and let's ChatGPT see what you are looking at.
Or you can choose share screen,
which lets ChatGPT view what is on your mobile screen
while you talk.
In this demo, I'll use screen sharing
to ask ChatGPT to review our lead with AI landing page.
First, I start a voice conversation
in the ChatGPT mobile app.
Then I tap the plus icon,
select share screen, and choose start broadcast.
Remember from this point,
ChatGPT can see what is showing on my mobile screen.
Now I move to the landing page on my phone.
Before I ask ChatGPT to review or suggest anything,
I start with a simple question.
Describe what you see on the screen.
This helps me check whether ChatGPT
is looking at the right information
and catch any misunderstandings early.
Once ChatGPT responds,
and I'm confident it understands the context,
I ask, can you review this landing page
and tell me if the value proposition is clear?
ChatGPT looks at the page and gives feedback
on the messaging, structure, and clarity.
From there, I can refine the conversation naturally.
For example,
turn this into a practical improvement plan,
including messaging, layout, and calls to action.
If I want to give ChatGPT more context
that isn't on the screen,
I can stop the screen share and switch to live video.
The conversation is documented in the chat,
so afterward, I can reread ChatGPT's observations,
feedback, and suggestions,
and turn the live discussion into notes, next steps,
or a follow-up action plan.
A few practical notes.
ChatGPT live video and screen sharing
are currently available only in the ChatGPT mobile app
and require a paid plan.
Be careful with what you share,
avoid confidential, personal, or sensitive information,
and always verify the output
before using it for any decisions.
The shift is simple.
Stop translating every visual situation into text.
Show ChatGPT the context directly,
then use your judgment to decide what to trust,
what to refine, and what to act on.
