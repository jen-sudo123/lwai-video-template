Microsoft Copilot starts in auto mode. For many tasks, that works perfectly well. Depending on your access, Copilot may 
offer additional models in the model picker in the Copilot chat window. So let's start with how the model picker works. 
In auto, Copilot automatically chooses the model it thinks best suits your prompt. For most inconsequential routine 
work like drafting, summarizing, rewriting, translation, and quick questions, this is usually enough. However, for 
higher stakes, external-facing, or complex work, advanced models can be very useful. Now, let's look at choosing a 
specific model. Open the model picker in your Copilot chat window. Depending on your interface, you may need to expand 
the menu to see additional choices. You may see options from OpenAI's GPT family and Anthropic's Claude family. The 
exact models available can change, so do not focus too much on memorizing every model name. What matters more is that 
different models handle the same task differently. For example, one may follow your instructions more closely. Another 
may structure the answer differently, handle a long document better, or produce writing that better matches the tone 
you want. Where your interface offers quick response and think deeper, use quick response for straightforward tasks and 
try think deeper when the task needs more analysis. More reasoning may take longer and does not guarantee a correct 
answer. So when might you try another model? You may want to manually choose another available model when the first 
result is not quite what you need, when you want a second approach, or when you want to compare how different models 
handle the same instructions. You may also have learned through experience that one model works particularly well for a 
certain type of task. The important thing is to keep the task the same when you compare. Use the same prompt, context, 
and source material. Then look at which model follows your instructions best and produces the most useful result. 
Finally, a note on access and pricing. The models you see depend on your Microsoft license, your organization's admin 
settings, and when Microsoft rolls out each model. Plans, model availability, and usage limits are subject to change. 
For the latest details, see Microsoft's official Copilot licensing guidance and subscription and usage-based billing 
guidance.
