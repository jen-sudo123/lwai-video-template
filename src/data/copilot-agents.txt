Copilot agents are reusable mini assistants
you can configure for a specific workflow.
Instead of re-explaining the same task
every time you open a new chat,
you can give the agent a clear role,
standing instructions, approved knowledge sources,
and optional capabilities.
Think of it as turning a super prompt
into a reusable assistant.
You can create agents from the Microsoft 365 Copilot app,
OneDrive or SharePoint,
depending on your license, permissions,
and organization settings.
