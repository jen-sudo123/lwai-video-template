[pause] The key difference is how the work is controlled. An AI agent can decide what to do next, based on its instructions, the user's request, and the information available.

[pause] An Agent Flow follows a process we've designed in advance. It can include AI-powered actions, but its overall sequence and rules are set ahead of time. This means we can combine the flexibility of AI reasoning with the consistency of a repeatable process.
