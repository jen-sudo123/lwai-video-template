## 4. Build brief

- **Engine:** Remotion, custom composition `src/compositions/DeepResearch.tsx`, ID `DeepResearch`, registered in `src/Root.tsx`.
- **Pattern:** `ClaudeSkills.tsx` / `SmallestAgent.tsx`: green `TitleCardGreen` "Deep Research" (90 frames), then
  `<Sequence from={90}>` with `t = frame / fps` = VO seconds; `<Audio src="audio/DeepResearch.mp3">` in the same sequence.
- **Scenes:** `const STARTS = [0, 10.08, 17.52, 25.36, 29.28, 42.36, 54.2, 62.88, 74.32, 81.76, 85.84, 96.04, 109.36, 122.16, 127.08, 140.08, 153.76, 168.4, 174.88, 186.88]`,
  `makeScene(STARTS)` crossfade 0.5s.
- **Duration:** 90 + round(197.0 × 30) = **6000 frames** (200s).
- **Kit pieces:** `Composer` (scenes 1, 7), `enter` / `markStyle` / `typed`, `Check` / `Cross` / `Arrow` / `FileIcon` icons, `.kit-card`, `.kit-pill`, `.kit-label`.
  New small SVG pieces: lock, unplugged connector, balance scale, timer.
- **Assets needed:** none beyond the VO. No screenshots, stock or AI generation.
- **Rules:** 80px safe margin, no bare em-dashes on screen, lime only as accent, reveal on the named word (~0.15s lead).
